THE COLLECTIVE

Built for the song. Not the spotlight.

The Affiliates Music is an independent songwriting and production collective focused on creating records that connect. From R&B and pop to gospel and beyond, we develop ideas, melodies and stories with one goal — finding the right song for the right artist.

BEHIND THE RECORDS

The Writers

SONGWRITER / PRODUCER

Jae Durr

R&B • POP • DANCE • GOSPEL • COUNTRY

Jae Durr is a songwriter and music producer creating across R&B, pop, dance, gospel and country. With a story-first approach to songwriting, he focuses on memorable concepts, melodies and records built around emotion.

SELECTED EXPERIENCE

DJ Maseo of De La Soul • KRS-One • Slip-N-Slide Records

SONGWRITER / SOUND ENGINEER

Ant Renoir

R&B • POP • GOSPEL • COUNTRY

Ant Renoir is a songwriter and SAE-trained sound engineer whose work bridges the creative and technical sides of record-making. Writing across R&B, pop, gospel and country, he brings a strong ear for songwriting, sound and the details that turn an idea into a finished record.

DJ Maseo of De La Soul • KRS-One • Slip-N-Slide Records
